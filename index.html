Orange Line OMS

An operations management system for the metro's maintenance and operations teams: work permits, power isolation, ground-wire orders, dispatch and maintenance plans.

Contact-center workforce management

Workforce AI

Forecasts call volume and generates agent shift schedules that cover demand with the fewest agents, for planners to review and edit.

Role
Built the forecasting and scheduling engine
Platform
Python, Flask, Angular, .NET

Context

The product needed demand forecasts and agent schedules generated from historical call data, then editable by planners in the dashboard.

What I built

  • Call-volume forecasting with Prophet from historical interval data.
  • An OR-Tools CP-SAT scheduler with continuous shift patterns, breaks and multi-day plans.
  • Soft demand constraints, so it always returns a schedule and reports any demand it could not cover.
  • A two-level objective: minimize unmet demand first, then the number of agents.
  • A Flask REST API for forecasts, schedules, edits and agents, packaged with Docker for the Angular dashboard.
  • Moved the .NET backend's data layer to PostgreSQL.
